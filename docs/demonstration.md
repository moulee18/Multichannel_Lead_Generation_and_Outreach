# Live Review & 8-Minute Technical Demonstration Guide

## Eight-Minute Technical Demo Sequence
1. **0:00 – 1:00 | Application Overview**: Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, show Overview KPIs, Country Distribution chart, Qualification Funnel, and 3-Agent status cards.
2. **1:00 – 2:15 | New Creator Discovery**: Navigate to **Creator Discovery**, select a niche keyword from the library, run live **Podcast RSS** discovery (or synthetic demo), and show pre-verification deduplication.
3. **2:15 – 3:30 | Evidence Verification**: Open **Creator Database**, click **Evidence Passport** on any creator to inspect source URLs and timestamps, then visit **Verification Queue** to resolve a `NEEDS_REVIEW` record.
4. **3:30 – 4:45 | Lead Scoring & Outreach**: Open **Lead Intelligence**, click **Score Breakdown** and **Channel Explainer**, then open **Outreach Studio** to inspect and edit 3-step sequences protected by the **AI Hallucination Guard**.
5. **4:45 – 5:45 | Smart Refresh with New Input**: Click **Smart Refresh** in **Agent Control Centre** and show how unchanged records are skipped and duplicates prevented.
6. **5:45 – 6:45 | Recovery from a Simple Failure**: In **Seed Test Lab**, enable *Simulate Transient Delivery Failure & Automatic Idempotent Recovery* and click *Test Unauthorised Recipient Block* to demonstrate error recovery and allowlist enforcement.
7. **6:45 – 8:00 | Quality Audit & Export**: In **Quality Audit**, run the **Random 20-Lead Audit Simulator** (`seed=42`), then open **Submission Centre** to review the 10-point Readiness Checklist and download the submission ZIP package.
