# IncuBrix Qualification & Verification Rules

A creator is marked `QUALIFIED` only when **every** check below passes with working public source evidence checked within the preceding 30 days:

1. **Real and Relevant**: Podcasters/video podcasters, YouTubers/video-first creators, or expert-led creators (coaches, consultants, educators). Excludes agencies, management firms, generic brands, publications, directories, and fan accounts.
2. **Active**: Either `>= 8` content items published within 30 days OR `>= 2` long-form items (`>= 600` seconds) published within 60 days.
3. **Commercial**: Public evidence of sponsorship, product, service, course, membership, subscription, or affiliate offer.
4. **Needs IncuBrix**: Public evidence of content creation, repurposing, editing, captions, publishing, consistency, backlog, or team workflow need.
5. **Country & Language**: Verified country metadata (at least 80% from US, CA, UK, IE, AU, NZ, SG; >= 3 countries; no single country > 60%; never guessed from name or accent) and mainly English recent content.
6. **Reachable**: Genuine public business contact channel supported by activity within the last 60 days (never guessed).
7. **Complete & Unique**: Evidence checked within 30 days and zero duplicate creators.
